- 小鼠脑：Ryan Kissinger / NIAID Visual & Medical Arts，[Mouse Brain](https://bioart.niaid.nih.gov/bioart/369)，Public Domain。样张缩放并裁去四周留白。

## 胆道诊断及五张医学插图样张

医学插画为 Servier Medical Art 经 Bioicons 提供的具体 [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) 素材；按样张需要缩放、组合和增加标注。所用素材为 [liver-3d](https://github.com/duerrsimon/bioicons/blob/main/static/icons/cc-by-3.0/Human_physiology/Servier/liver-3d.svg)、[pancreas-2](https://github.com/duerrsimon/bioicons/blob/main/static/icons/cc-by-3.0/Human_physiology/Servier/pancreas-2.svg)、[aorta-body](https://github.com/duerrsimon/bioicons/blob/main/static/icons/cc-by-3.0/Human_physiology/Servier/aorta-body.svg)、[heart-front](https://github.com/duerrsimon/bioicons/blob/main/static/icons/cc-by-3.0/Human_physiology/Servier/heart-front.svg)、[runner](https://github.com/duerrsimon/bioicons/blob/main/static/icons/cc-by-3.0/Human_physiology/Servier/runner.svg)、[kidney-1](https://github.com/duerrsimon/bioicons/blob/main/static/icons/cc-by-3.0/Human_physiology/Servier/kidney-1.svg) 和 [venous-circulation-body](https://github.com/duerrsimon/bioicons/blob/main/static/icons/cc-by-3.0/Human_physiology/Servier/venous-circulation-body.svg)。
